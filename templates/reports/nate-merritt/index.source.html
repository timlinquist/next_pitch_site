05

Delayed Hips / Shoulder

What we look for

Hips begin to open while the shoulders stay closed longer, creating separation (torque). The delay stores energy for a late, efficient upper-half fire.

06

Swivel & Stabilize

What we look for

Front foot lands and the lead leg braces/stabilizes so the pelvis can swivel against a firm front side. Stability here protects timing and arm health.
